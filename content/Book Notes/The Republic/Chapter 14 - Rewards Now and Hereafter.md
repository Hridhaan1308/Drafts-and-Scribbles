The book concludes by discussing the eternal rewards of morality.

The mind, which is also the life-force, is declared **immortal** because its specific evil (immorality/badness) only deforms it, but cannot destroy it.

The **Myth of Er** describes afterlife judgment and the process of **reincarnation**. The most important task for a soul is to acquire the "competence and knowledge" to rationally choose the better life lot for the future.

The ultimate reward is not just external prizes, but the perpetuation of the **"rational orderliness of goodness"** in oneself, which is the moral mission of the whole text. The message is to always **"keep to the upward path"**.
