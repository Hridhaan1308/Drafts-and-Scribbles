The community is formally divided into Rulers (Guardians Proper), Auxiliaries (military/police), and Workers, justified by the **Noble Lie**.

The Guardians must live communally, receive only provisions as a stipend, and **possess no private property**. This prevents them from becoming "estate-managers and farmers instead of guardians".

[[Socrates]] addresses the claim that this austere life is unhappy. He responds that the aim is to **"maximize the happiness of the community as a whole,"** not to make any single group uniquely happy in a material sense.

I think the emphasis on communal unity and the sacrifice of personal wealth confirms the psychological objective. The idea is that the highest part of my mind (Reason) should not be motivated by money-making or acquisition (the desirous part), but by the health of the entire psychological system.
