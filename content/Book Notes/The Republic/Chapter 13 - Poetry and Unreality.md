The final, rigorous philosophical attack on poetry.
1. **Metaphysical Objection:** Representational poetry is far removed from the truth, standing **two steps away from reality**. The painter or poet creates mere "appearances, not reality".
2. **Psychological Objection:** Poetry **"deforms its audience’s minds"** because it appeals directly to, and **"feeds and fattens up,"** the emotional and irrational part of the mind. By encouraging the indulgence of feelings (like excessive grief or laughter) that we normally restrain, it "establishes a bad system of government in people’s minds".

Only **"hymns to the gods and eulogies of virtuous men"** are permissible, as philosophy must serve as an **"incantation"** to protect the rational soul.

The banishment of the poets is ultimately an ethical measure to preserve the internal order, Morality. Poetry is a serious threat because it directly undermines the rational control established in Chapter 6.
