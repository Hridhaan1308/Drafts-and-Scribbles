[[Plato]] fulfills the final task: comparing the aristocratic system and its human counterpart with four degenerate forms to assess happiness.
1. **Timocracy:** Ruled by **Passion/Ambition**.
2. **Oligarchy:** Ruled by **Necessary Desires** (money/wealth).
3. **Democracy:** Ruled by **Unnecessary Desires** (excessive freedom/licentiousness).
4. **Dictatorship/Tyranny:** Ruled by **Lawless Desires**.

The **Dictator** is the most unhappy person because his mind is fully enslaved to his lust, filled with inconsistency and fear. The mind is depicted as a **Human (reason), a Lion (passion), and a mutable, many-headed Monster (desire)**. Morality means the Human governs and nurtures the whole composite.

I think the definition of immorality as psychic disintegration is powerful. It provides the necessary counter-argument to Thrasymachus: the dictator's outwardly powerful life is an inwardly miserable one, making morality intrinsically superior.
