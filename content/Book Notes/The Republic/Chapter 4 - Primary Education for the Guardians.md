The Guardians’ education, consisting of physical exercise and cultural studies, is intended to temper their spirited and gentle natures.

**Censorship of Content:** Literature must be rigorously controlled. Stories must never portray God as changing, lying, or causing evil, or heroes as grieving excessively or laughing uncontrollably. This is because early exposure to badness leads to internal corruption.

**Censorship of Form:** The educational system also bans extensive **representation (impersonation)**, as this violates the PS. By mimicking too many different roles or characters, one risks adopting an "inferior stamp" and undermines one's adherence to one's own nature.

This primary education is largely **indoctrination**. It ensures the Guardians have **true belief** (that certain things are to be feared, etc.) and fosters psychic harmony, but it doesn't yet equip them for the profound intellectual inquiry required of true philosophers.
