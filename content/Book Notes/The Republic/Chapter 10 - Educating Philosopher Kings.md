The higher intellectual curriculum is laid out to lead students to the truth and compel the mind to think abstractly.

The subjects selected (**Arithmetic, Geometry, Solid Geometry, Astronomy, Harmonics**) are chosen because they force the mind away from the deceptive senses ("the world of becoming") and toward stable, **immaterial reality** ("numbers in themselves").

The capstone is **Dialectic**: the ability to give and understand explanations, grasp the interconnections of all subjects, and ascend using pure reason to the ultimate starting point (Goodness).

The training is selective and lengthy: five years of dialectic (age 30–35), followed by fifteen years of practical experience and warfare (age 35–50). Only those who emerge unscathed from all physical and intellectual tests are "guided to the climax of their lives" to view goodness.

I think this is about creating incorruptible reluctance. The philosopher's true wealth is "a life of virtue and intelligence," which is "preferable to ruling". They must be forced to rule as an obligation, not a privilege.
