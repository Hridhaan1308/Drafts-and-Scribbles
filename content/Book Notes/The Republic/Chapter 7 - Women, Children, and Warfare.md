[[Socrates]] addresses the two most controversial political "waves"
1. **Equality of Women:** Female Guardians must receive **"identical education and duties"** as men, based purely on the PS—if a woman's nature equips her for a task, gender is irrelevant.
2. **Communal Living:** The Guardian class must have **no private marriage, wives, or children**. Children are shared and considered children of the whole class. This ensures maximum **unity** by preventing members from calling different things "mine" and creating private pleasures and pains.

Regarding warfare, Greeks fighting other Greeks should be termed **"conflict"** (internal dissension), not "war" (external hostility), and they should avoid devastating Greek land or burning homes, aiming for reconciliation.

I think the extreme nature of the proposals (especially communal living) should be read as reinforcing the theme of **unity**. This "hard" proposal serves a "soft" purpose: the highest parts of the soul (Reason/Passion) must be completely detached from private, possessive motivations (the Desirous/Mercenary part) to rule justly.
